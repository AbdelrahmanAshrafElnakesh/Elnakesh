Integration of NDIR Sensing, GPS and Multi-Gyroscope Modules for Real-Time In-Vehicle Alcohol Detection and Driver Safety Enhancement

This work presents a fused sensing approach for the cabin environment: non-dispersive infrared (NDIR) measurement supports alcohol-related detection, GPS provides location and motion context, and multiple gyroscopes improve orientation and stability estimates—together enabling safer, more informed in-vehicle monitoring and driver protection.
